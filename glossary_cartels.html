Built by TransGalMeg Industries, the Rihkxyrk (rik-zerk) is a brutal, overbuilt assault fighter designed to win engagements through durability and firepower rather than speed. Large, expensive, and slow on the approach, it compensates with thick armor, powerful shields, and excellent maneuverability, capable of matching frontline starfighters such as the X-wing in a turning fight. Too costly for widespread adoption by major militaries, the Rihkxyrk instead became a favorite of pirates, criminal syndicates, and well-funded planetary militias during the Galactic Civil War. In their hands, it earned a reputation for bullying lighter craft and standing its ground against more refined designs, provided it avoided being swarmed or outmaneuvered. When flown aggressively and supported properly, the Rihkxyrk is a difficult target to bring down and a dangerous presence on any battlefield.

A product of budget over function, the I-Wing is a crudely built, mass-produced snubfighter favored by pirates, raiders, and fringe warlords. Its distinctive single-wing profile mounts a twin light laser cannon on one end and a compact cockpit-engine module on the other. Though its frame lacks a hyperdrive and is protected only by a weak shield generator and minimal armor, the I-Wing makes up for it with agility, swarm tactics, and one uncommon feature: its laser cannon is mounted in a swiveling turret. This allows it to track targets slightly above, below, or to either side of the craft, granting it about 80 degrees of traverse independent of the pilot’s direct line of sight.

So poorly protected that even the Rebel Alliance refused to field them, I-Wings are often seen as expendable tools rather than true starfighters. They're primarily used in overwhelming numbers, hoping sheer volume can make up for individual fragility. They're so cheap, a full squadron of I-Wings can be purchased for the price of a single TIE fighter, making them a tempting option for criminal syndicates and warlords looking to flood the battlefield on a budget.

The Hutt-designed Karakta-class Turbo-Sailer is an unusual craft that functions as a mobile heavy weapons platform. A large solar sail feeds power to a central reactor, allowing the main weapon to be configured as either a starship-grade ion cannon or a rapid-fire turbolaser. The MandalMotors M22-T Krayt Gunship was originally commissioned by Jabba the Hutt as a high-performance command ship for his lieutenants. Its oversized engine grants exceptional speed for a vessel of its size, while Mandalorian design philosophy favors heavy forward-facing armament, including ion cannons, repeating lasers, and high-explosive and armor-piercing missiles. Developed by Kuat Systems Engineering for the Republic, the R-22 “Spearhead” was designed to replace the V-19, Delta-7, and Eta-2 as part of a broader shift toward independent starfighter doctrine. Unlike its predecessors, the R-22 combined shielding, extreme maneuverability, and a compact profile with an integrated hyperdrive, eliminating the need for docking rings and allowing it to operate independently across multiple theaters. This made the Spearhead well suited to the Republic’s growing reliance on fast-deploying fleets and deep-space operations.

As the Clone Wars drew to a close, the program was abandoned, with the Imperial Navy rejecting it in favor of the cheaper TIE line built around mass production and carrier reliance. Diverted to civilian and black market sales, the R-22 found new life among rebel cells, criminal syndicates, and independent factions. With exceptional speed, agility, respectable shielding, and repeating laser cannons capable of extremely high rates of fire, it proved more than a match for contemporary starfighters, and its combat record would later influence Kuat’s next generation of interceptors, culminating in the RZ-1 A-Wing.

A product of a rare and dangerous collaboration between the Hutt Cartels and the Black Sun syndicate, the Privateer-class Battleship was designed with one goal in mind: overwhelming firepower. Built to surpass traditional Hutt warships such as the Chelandion Heavy Cruiser, the Privateer is a sleek, fast, and surprisingly compact vessel for its class. Six heavy proton torpedo launchers mounted in the forward hull provide devastating opening strikes, while a network of turbolasers and medium ion cannons ensure effective coverage in the firing dead zones of its primary armament.

The true strength of the Privateer lies in its eight long-range but slow firing 1000mm mass driver cannons, mounted in broadside configuration with four turrets per flank. Half are configured to fire armor-piercing rounds, while the remainder launch high-explosive projectiles. Though equipped with several heavy laser cannons for limited self-defense, the Privateer remains highly vulnerable to bomber attacks due to its lack of a starfighter complement and restricted firing arcs. A true battleship in every sense, it is best employed behind the front lines under escort, where it can devastate enemy fleets from extreme range.
