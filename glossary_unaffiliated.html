The 181st Imperial Fighter Wing, commanded by Baron Soontir Fel, is composed of some of the finest pilots in the Imperial Navy and is widely regarded as one of the deadliest starfighter formations in the galaxy. Renowned for its discipline, coordination, and ruthless efficiency in close combat, the wing has earned a fearsome reputation among Rebel pilots, many of whom consider an encounter with the 181st a near-certain death sentence. The unit is organized into four squadrons of twelve craft each, including three fighter squadrons (Alpha, Beta, and Saber) and one bomber squadron (Gamma), allowing it to conduct sustained operations while maintaining overwhelming pressure in any engagement.

Armor: Heavy Weapons: x1 Twin Light Laser Cannon, x2 Armor-Piercing Missile Launcher, x2 Advanced Proton Torpedo Launcher, x2 Ion Cannon, x6 Light Laser Cannon

Elite Fighter Commander: The Baron and his 181st Wing inspire nearby fighters, boosting armor, shields, damage, and speed by 25%.

Always stacks with other fleet commander bonuses.
