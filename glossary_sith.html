After being crippled in the aftermath of the Jedi Civil War, the Dying Sun fell to Nyriaan, shattering across the planet’s surface. Glovoc entered a deep stasis while his marooned crew devolved over generations, inbreeding into a cult of deranged worshippers. Over the millennia, fragments of the ship were slowly reassembled, until the Infinite Engine stirred once more, regenerating the command ship beneath their settlement. When Glovoc awoke, his people rose with him, twisted zealots ready to return their master to the stars.

The Dying Sun is a corrupted Interdictor-class Star Destroyer, rebuilt around a fragment of the Star Forge that has become an Infinite Engine. This dark artifact grants the ship regenerative capabilities and endless reserves of ancient Sith starfighters, while its fanatical crew, bound to the will of Darth Glovoc, will sacrifice all reason to fuel his cause. In battle, Glovoc can drive the ship into a suicidal frenzy, overloading its reactor to channel immense power into its weapons, temporarily boosting firepower at the cost of self-preservation.
