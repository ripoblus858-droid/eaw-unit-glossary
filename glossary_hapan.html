A high-performance starfighter designed by the Hapes Consortium, the Miy’Tok features an advanced power management system and dual solar sails that generate excess energy for its engines, shields, and weapons. This allows for extremely high speed, rapidly recharging shields, and repeating laser cannons capable of sustained, uninterrupted fire. These advantages come at a cost. The Miy’Tok’s handling is mediocre, and its large profile and light armor make it an easy target for enemies that manage to get behind it.

This unit is unaffected by nebulas.

The Miy’Til Bomber is a fast, durable heavy strike craft deployed by the Hapes Consortium for precision attacks against enemy capital ships. Emphasizing speed and survivability, it can outrun most starfighters, especially when diverting additional power to its already powerful engines. Designed for rapid hit-and-run strikes, the Miy’Til can heavily debuff enemy shields and armor to support friendly fighters, or remain on target to inflict serious damage with its heavy laser cannons. Best employed against large warships, it excels at weakening hostile vessels and creating openings for the rest of the Hapan Starfleet to exploit.

This unit is unaffected by nebulas.
