The Basilisk War Droid is an ancient Mandalorian assault craft, revived in modern form as a fast, heavily armored strike platform. Its forward-facing weapons suite is designed for overwhelming close-range attacks, allowing it to tear through lightly armored starfighters with devastating volleys. Foldable wings grant bursts of speed for rapid ambushes or quick disengagements, making the Basilisk a dangerous hit-and-run attacker.

True to Mandalorian Neo-Crusader doctrine, the Basilisk sacrifices shields and pilot protection in favor of brute offensive power and durability. While vulnerable to sustained fire from heavier warships, these semi-sentient war machines excel when unleashed against fighter screens, where their concentrated firepower can shred entire squadrons in moments.

The Mandal Hypernautics StarViper Attack Platform (SVAP-2) is the mass-producible evolution of Prince Xizor’s personal starfighter, Virago. While the Virago was meant to be the only craft of its kind, Mandal Hypernautics reacquired the production rights and designed a version suitable for mass production: the SVAP-2. Although the standard StarViper is less powerful, it remains an astonishingly advanced and capable starfighter, with the price tag to match. Extremely maneuverable thanks to its armored maneuvering fins, and highly durable due to strong shields and beskar-inlaid armor, the StarViper also features an advanced targeting system for its swiveling weapon mounts, allowing the craft to maintain an accurate and wide field of fire.

While durable, the large StarViper presents a substantial frontal target profile, making it vulnerable to even inaccurate weaponry when its S-foils are locked in the attack position. It is especially susceptible to missiles and torpedoes, relying on countermeasure flares for temporary protection. Despite this vulnerability, the StarViper is a top-of-the-line assault fighter capable of engaging a variety of targets and excelling in dogfights.

The MandalMotors M22-T Krayt Gunship was originally commissioned by Jabba the Hutt as a high-performance command ship for his lieutenants. Like the krayt dragon it was named after, the M22 is built for hunting. Its oversized engine grants exceptional speed for a vessel of its size, while Mandalorian design philosophy favors heavy forward-facing armament, including ion cannons, repeating lasers, and high-explosive and armor-piercing missiles. This concentrated firepower allows the Krayt to aggressively pursue and overwhelm a wide range of targets.

For self-defense, the M22 is protected by strong shields and a quad light laser turret providing broad defensive coverage. However, the majority of its weapons are fixed forward, requiring the Krayt to line up attack runs much like a starfighter. This limited firing arc is a significant weakness for a ship of its size, and it remains vulnerable to sustained missile and torpedo strikes.
